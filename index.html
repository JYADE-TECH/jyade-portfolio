About the Designer

I am Justine Yuri De Leon, an Architectural Drafting & Graphics Design Technology student at Bulacan State University and the founder of JYADE — a full-service design and construction consultancy.

My work bridges the gap between vision and structure. I believe that every space tells a story — through form, proportion, material, and light. I bring technical precision and creative clarity to every project I take on.

Whether you need a full house plan, a renovation concept, or detailed structural drawings, I deliver work that is grounded, considered, and built to last.
